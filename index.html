privacy policy

this policy explains what opair collects when you request an invite and how that information is used.

information we collect

when you submit this form, we collect your first name, last name, phone number, zip code, and whether you're joining as a parent or a babysitter.

how we use it

we use this information to review invite requests, contact you about your request, and, if approved, to set up your opair account. we do not sell your information to third parties.

how we store it

information is stored securely and retained only as long as needed to process your request or, if you join, to operate your account.

your choices

you can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us directly.
